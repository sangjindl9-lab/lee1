EUM Purchase Note Privacy Policy

Summary

EUM Purchase Note (“the app”) does not collect personal data or send it anywhere. There is no sign-up, login, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics.

Your records and photos never leave your phone. The internet is used only for “Import from a shopping site” below.

Import from a shopping site

The app opens the official site of a shop you choose (e.g. Coupang, Naver, Amazon) in an in-app browser. You sign in on that site yourself; the app never reads or saves your ID or password.

Only when you tap “Import this page”, the text of the order page you are viewing is read on your phone and turned into purchase records. It is not sent to our server.

Traffic with the shopping site and its sign-in state (cookies) follow that shop's privacy policy. You can clear them any time in Settings > “Clear shopping site sign-ins”.

Import rules (how to read shop pages) are downloaded from this site. No personal data is sent.

What the app stores on your phone

Purchase details you enter or choose: product name, store, order date, amount and currency, order number, store link, memo, return/exchange/repair deadlines, status and reminder settings.

Copies of photos you share or pick, and the text read from them on the phone.

This stays in the app's private storage; we cannot see it. It is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Photos and text recognition

The app only reads files you share with it or pick in the system photo picker. It does not ask for access to all photos or storage, and never deletes or changes originals in your gallery.

Text is read on the phone with Google ML Kit's on-device models bundled in the app; photos and recognized text are not sent anywhere. ML Kit's usage-statistics upload has been removed from the app.

Permissions

Internet: only for importing from shopping sites and downloading import rules.

Notifications (optional): to send the deadline reminders you set. If you decline, you can still see deadlines in the app. The lock screen shows only a version without product name or order number.

Run at startup (boot completed): to re-schedule reminders after the phone restarts.

Backup

Only when you ask, the app writes a password-encrypted backup file (AES-256) to a location you choose. If that location is a cloud service, that service's policy applies. If you forget the password, nobody can restore it.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your phone until you delete it or uninstall the app. Use Settings > “Delete all data” at any time.

If you uninstall without a backup file, your records cannot be recovered.

Sharing with third parties

We do not receive your data, so we do not share or sell it.

Children

The app does not ask for age and is not designed primarily for children. It collects no data from users of any age.

This web page

The server that hosts this page (Railway) may keep standard access logs (IP address, time, requested URL) for operations. This page uses no cookies or analytics.
