Help & contact

How do I save an order?

Open the order in your shopping app, tap Share and choose “EUM Purchase Note”. For a screenshot, open it in your gallery and share it. You can also tap “Add purchase” and type it in.

The text was read wrong

Nothing is filled in automatically. Tap the correct suggestion or fix it yourself. You can also change “Text language” and read again.

Are deadlines set automatically?

No. Only dates written on the receipt that you confirm, or dates you enter, are saved. If the start date is unclear (e.g. “within 7 days of delivery”) the deadline is left empty.

Reminders are late or missing

Battery saving can delay them. If notifications are off, turn them on in Settings. Deadlines are always shown on the home screen.

Changing phones

Make an encrypted backup in Settings > Backup and restore, move the file to the new phone and restore it. Without the password it can't be restored.

Deleting my data

Tap Settings > “Delete all data”. Photos in your gallery are not deleted.
