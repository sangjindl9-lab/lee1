EUM Kids Coloring Book
[short]
30 cute pictures to color. No ads, works offline, safe for kids.
[full]
EUM Kids Coloring is a coloring book app for young children with 30 original pictures.

■ 30 pictures in 5 themes
Animal friends, dinosaur friends, robots and space, fantasy friends and vehicle friends.

■ Easy for little hands
· Tap an area to fill it with color
· Brush, pencil, crayon, marker and glitter
· "Stay inside the lines" keeps the color in its area
· Stickers, undo and redo
· Auto color and color ideas with matching color sets

■ Made for phones and tablets
· Works with a stylus (S Pen and others), including pen pressure
· Pinch to zoom in on small areas

■ Safe for children
· No ads, no sign-up, no in-app purchases
· Works offline and has no internet permission
· Collects no data
· Sharing and the parents menu open only after a grown-up check

■ Keeps every picture
· Saves automatically while your child colors
· Finished pictures stay in "My art" and can be exported as photos

The pictures were created with AI image generation (Higgsfield).
